# Responsible gambling posture

HollerSports is **not a sportsbook** and does not take bets. Real-world wagering—if you do any—is entirely outside this software.

## Product design that supports safer use

- **Paper only** — no deposits, withdrawals, or book placement  
- **Fail closed** — missing odds/lines/provenance do not invent certainty  
- **Simulation labels** — stakes / PnL / ROI are advice-quality metrics  
- **No “Place bet” UX** — CI and runtime guards forbid live-wagering labels  

## If you wager elsewhere

- Only bet what you can afford to lose  
- Set time and money limits with your licensed operator  
- Do not chase losses  
- Seek help if gambling stops being fun or becomes compulsive  

## Help resources (examples)

| Region | Resource |
|--------|----------|

| Self-exclusion | Use tools offered by licensed operators in your jurisdiction |

## App Store / marketing rules of thumb

- Never claim the app “makes you money” or “beats the books”  
- Never show fabricated win rates in screenshots  
- Always pair any performance chart with **sample size**, **paper/sim**, and **past performance ≠ future results**  
- Prefer “market intelligence / paper simulation” over “betting app” in store subtitle unless counsel approves gambling category copy
