# Age and jurisdiction policy

## Age floor

| Context | Minimum age |
|---------|-------------|
| **Default product policy** | **18+** |
| **Where real-money sports betting is 21+** (e.g. many US states) | Treat **21+** as the practical floor for any user who will act on advice offline |
| **App Store age rating (when listed)** | Expect **17+** (Frequent/Intense Simulated Gambling **or** references to real-world gambling). Confirm with App Store Connect questionnaire at submission time. |

The Workbench first-run gate requires an explicit acknowledgment of the age floor before use.

This product is **not** directed at children and must not be listed in Kids categories.

## Jurisdiction

Sports wagering laws vary by country, state, and municipality. HollerSports:

- Does **not** verify geo-license for real-money play (it does not offer real-money play)  
- Still requires operators and end users to **comply with local law** when using market data or when betting elsewhere  
- Must not be marketed as legal advice or as a licensed sportsbook  

If you ship a consumer build into restricted regions, obtain counsel on:

- Whether “betting tips / odds tools” are regulated as gambling services  
- Required disclaimers and age gates  
- Whether distribution is allowed at all  

## Operator deployments

Self-hosted operators remain responsible for access control (who can open the Workbench) and for not exposing the stack to under-age users on shared devices.
