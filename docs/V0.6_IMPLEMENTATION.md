# HollerSports V0.6 implementation status

V0.6 is implemented as bounded, shadow-only domains beside the unchanged V0.5
pipelines. V0.5 remains the control group.

## Implemented foundation

- Append-only `market_snapshot.v2` JSONL memory with stable fingerprints and
  temporal replay. Every quote requires a source identifier and source hash,
  snapshot IDs are unique, and timezone-aware observation times cannot exceed
  their snapshot capture time.
- Deterministic multiplicative, additive, power, and explicit-parameter Shin
  de-vig; weighted cross-book consensus with concentration diagnostics;
  pricing residual; and lead/lag helpers. Unsupported `BOOK_CALIBRATED`
  reconstruction fails closed until calibration evidence is supplied.
- Evidence-available de-vig backtests compare raw implied probability against all
  deterministic methods using multiclass Brier and log loss, scoped by bookmaker,
  sport, hierarchical market class, and odds bucket. A preferred method is exposed
  only after the configured minimum sample and only when it beats raw implied Brier;
  malformed method parameters fail that method closed rather than aborting the run.
- Minimum-sample bookmaker fingerprints with per-field computability and source
  provenance. Each fingerprint field must independently meet the configured
  sample floor. Qualified fingerprints can be promoted into an append-only,
  market-class-specific ledger whose evidence rows are content-fingerprinted;
  historical replay gates on both evidence availability and promotion time, and
  integrity health fails closed on malformed or tampered packets.
- Longitudinal bookmaker timelines are scoped by both event and market identity
  and report raw-implied probability velocity and threshold velocity, explicitly
  configured move events, nearest-predecessor observed leader/follower lags, public-handle
  imbalance, elasticity, and resistance observations. Pressure causes remain
  explicitly `UNKNOWN`; tickets and handle are never labeled as sharp action.
  Timeline movement is labeled `RAW_IMPLIED_OBSERVATION` and is not substituted
  for the separately reconstructed fair-probability path. Movement, lead/lag, and
  pressure each expose an independent `COMPUTED`/`NOT_COMPUTABLE` evidence gate.
- Closing-price and settled-outcome backtests for Brier score, log loss, ECE,
  fair calibration error, book pricing error, CLV information capture, and
  verdict discrimination. Every metric reports its usable sample size.
  Consensus rejects incomparable selection sets and reports market disagreement
  entropy separately from bookmaker-weight concentration entropy.
- Append-only, provenance-complete prop ladder observations and separately
  fingerprinted derived surfaces with temporal replay. Surface reconstruction
  reports monotonic-repair residuals, fit error, in-range interpolated quantiles,
  bounded quantile moments, ladder curvature, tail-tax, and named pairwise
  distribution conflicts.
- Settled prop-surface backtests now emit Gate D's monotonic-violation rate and
  calibration error alongside threshold-level Brier and a threshold-integrated,
  observed-range-normalized discrete CRPS approximation.
  Evaluation is isolated by market class and model ID, excludes settlements not
  available by the replay cutoff, and binds every forecast to pre-prediction model
  provenance without silently repairing inversions before scoring them.
- Evidence-bound player, travel, recovery, coaching, opportunity, and venue
  context transforms. Context observations and derivations are now persisted
  separately as append-only, fingerprint-linked packets with temporal replay;
  replay gates on both evidence observation time and repository recording time,
  and API writes use a process lock with rollback to avoid partial bundles;
  context health revalidates duplicate IDs, source fingerprints, identity fields,
  evidence provenance, and derivation signal bindings and fails closed on unreadable
  or tampered local ledgers;
  API responses keep context, player, bookmaker, market, and dependency evidence
  in distinct fields.
- Inspectable dependency graphs, explicit-opt-in independence, empirical
  conditional and shared-driver joint probability methods, correlation-tax
  decomposition, and marginal-leg analysis. Dependency observations and their
  deterministic parlay analyses are persisted as fingerprint-linked append-only
  bundles with recorded-time replay gates, rollback, integrity health checks,
  and evidence-separated API replay. Core graph analysis also rejects duplicate
  legs, dangling or duplicate edges, and self-dependencies before computing a
  joint probability.
- Settled joint-probability backtests compare modeled and explicit-independent
  Brier/log-loss on evidence available by the replay cutoff. They enforce the
  marginal cap and exact independent product, reject duplicate evidence and mixed
  leg counts, bind every forecast to immutable pre-prediction model evidence and a
  specific model ID, scope by market class and model method, and expose the exact Gate E
  log-loss improvement without automatically declaring release readiness.
- Calibration-owned deterministic structural verdicts with normal `PASS` and
  `NOT_COMPUTABLE` outcomes. Evidence references, calibration-threshold identity,
  observations, and deterministic verdict derivations are persisted separately
  with temporal replay and integrity checks.
- Explicit release-readiness gates A–H fail closed on missing evidence and test
  temporal replay, de-vig improvement, qualified fingerprints, prop calibration,
  joint-model improvement, verdict discrimination, PASS calibration improvement,
  and the unchanged V0.5 regression control. Every computable gate requires an
  explicit evidence provenance reference; synthetic metrics without provenance
  remain `NOT_COMPUTABLE`.
- Gate B, D, and E metrics can now be assembled only from validated de-vig,
  prop-surface, and joint-probability backtest packets. The assembler fingerprints
  each complete packet and discards caller-supplied unbound scalar replacements.
- Positive, minimum-sample de-vig calibration winners can be promoted into a
  scoped append-only shadow ledger with temporal replay and packet integrity
  checks. Learned player-outcome, context, dependency, and joint model artifacts
  have a separate append-only shadow promotion registry with artifact/evidence
  hashes, ordered training/evaluation/promotion times, minimum-sample enforcement,
  and a local hash chain. Promotion never activates execution or capital authority.
- A read-only historical backfill auditor inventories fixture and V0.6 evidence,
  rejects provenance-incomplete rows, checks market-to-settlement event overlap and
  temporal ordering, and reports whether V0.5 fixture replay or V0.6 temporal replay
  is currently possible. It performs no writes and cannot authorize a backfill.
- Independent FastAPI endpoints for market memory, singles, prop surfaces,
  parlay X-Ray/Surgery, and health.

All packets remain `SHADOW_ONLY` or `ADVISORY_ONLY`, with
`capital_authority=false` and `execution_authority=false`.

## Deliberately not claimed yet

- Book fingerprints are not emitted or promoted as computed without configured
  historical sample thresholds. Promotion requires immutable evidence-row hashes,
  an explicit hierarchical market class, and evidence availability timestamps.
- No universal de-vig method is selected. The current deterministic endpoint
  labels proportional de-vig as `MULTIPLICATIVE`. Backtest winners remain scoped
  diagnostics and can be registered only as shadow promotions. They are never
  automatically activated in analysis, and callers remain responsible for proving
  the supplied sample is independently held out rather than training evidence.
- Model promotion registries now exist for context, dependency, joint, and player
  outcome families. No learned artifact is bundled or promoted by default, and
  prop storage still does not claim a trained player-distribution model.
- Prop quantiles and moments never extrapolate outside observed thresholds. They
  are bounded diagnostics rather than a fully identified continuous outcome
  distribution, and tail classifications are descriptive rather than calibrated
  recommendation thresholds.
- Local JSONL packets use stable fingerprints and duplicate-ID guards, but do not
  yet provide an external signed manifest or cryptographic hash chain. Likewise,
  `source_hash` is retained as provider provenance and cannot be verified unless
  the original source bytes are available to the ingestion adapter.
- Learned book-calibrated, player-outcome, and joint-outcome models are not yet
  promoted.
- Observed longitudinal leader/follower counts are descriptive event-timeline
  evidence, not a learned bookmaker-information-leadership fingerprint. Promotion
  still requires closing-price calibration and minimum historical samples by
  sport and market class.
- V0.6 is not an advisory beta until the closing-price and settled-outcome
  release gates in the specification have independent held-out evidence.

## Validation

Tests cover probability bounds, paired fair-probability normalization,
monotonic prop surfaces, joint probability marginal caps, unknown dependency
fail-closed behavior, immutable append-only observations, temporal replay,
governance locks, V0.6 API workflows, and the full existing V0.5 suite.
